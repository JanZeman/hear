# World 2 — The Paper Garden

## Technology target

**2.5D**.

2.5D here means predominantly 2D art layers placed at different depths in a 3D scene so camera movement produces real parallax.

## Mood

Japanese-inspired paper / puppet / shadow theatre. Elegant, adult-friendly, handcrafted, quiet but not sterile.

Do cultural research before production art; avoid a generic collage of unrelated motifs.

## Layer concept

Example layer stack:

```text
foreground curtains / lanterns
cherry branch
character / puppet layer
zen garden
bridge / water
architecture
mountains
paper sky
```

Layers should have generous horizontal and vertical bleed for responsive reframing.

## Success events

Correct detections can advance a curated sequence of small theatrical actions rather than repeating one animation:

- gardener makes a rake stroke,
- paper crane flies,
- puppet crosses a bridge,
- cat changes position,
- sliding doors open,
- another small stage action.

These occur only after outcome classification.

## Session progression

A small performance/story unfolds with normalized session progress even if some tones are missed. Success events enrich the performance.

## Ear-aware presentation

Optional: choose a left-side or right-side stage action after classification. Do not pre-position or animate a side-specific cue before the response.
