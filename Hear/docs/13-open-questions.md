# Open questions — do not silently invent final answers

These are intentionally not frozen yet.

## Product / measurement

- Exact audiometric protocol and calibration semantics across arbitrary devices/headphones.
- Exact result presentation and any population-reference mapping.
- How confidently headphone use can be detected on each platform vs explicitly selected by the user.
- Final behavior for misses/false positives in each world (including whether some remain visually silent).

## UX

- Exact shell typography/font family.
- Exact icon set.
- Exact minimum desktop window size and responsive breakpoints.
- Whether Results deserves primary navigation from day one.
- Exact settings surface.

## Brand

- Final optical dimensions and spacing of the HEAR mark.
- Final Companion silhouette and name.
- Exact logo-to-Companion reveal timing.

## World art

- Production asset source strategy: generated, modeled, purchased, procedural or mixed.
- Final cultural/art research for Paper Garden and any future culturally specific world.
- Final settlement/cultural direction for River Journey.

When implementation requires one of these decisions, prefer a reversible placeholder and document it rather than hard-coding an invented product decision.
