# HEAR visual language

## Brand lockup

Working lockup:

```text
  •        ellipse     ellipse       ellipse
  H          E           A             R
          Sound opens worlds.
```

The four symbol elements align to the centers of H, E, A and R.

### Color direction

- H element: very dark navy dot.
- E element: pearl / pale lavender-blue.
- A element: soft sky / cool blue.
- R element: blue with subtle aurora violet.
- Default state is light and restrained.
- Active states may temporarily become more saturated.

## Motion identity

Static logo remains elliptical.

When animated, ellipses may:

- expand from the dot,
- open into curved wave-like forms,
- act as a transition portal,
- eventually give physical volume to the final ellipse.

Do not make the static mark look like a generic speaker-volume icon.

## Companion

- Separate from the logo.
- Derived later from the final ellipse.
- Pearl-white/light body with subtle aurora tint.
- Friendly but not babyish.
- Eyes/features should be minimal so the character does not become primarily a symbol of vision.
- Body language should communicate listening: tilt, lean, orient, subtle signal accents.
- Keep the shape abstract and organic; never reproduce product-specific hardware details.
- The Companion can adapt to worlds (paper version, hat, space gear, etc.) while keeping the same silhouette/DNA.

## One strong rule

**Logo does not dress for each world. Companion may.**
