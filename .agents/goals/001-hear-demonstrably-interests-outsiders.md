## Why these numbers

Ten is the owner's own deliberately small, manually observable bar for genuine outside interest at
this early, single-person-demo stage, not a figure derived from an external benchmark. The point
is a real go/no-go signal from people who have no reason to be polite about it, not a statistically
meaningful sample.

## Trade-offs with the other objectives

None yet: this is the Venture's only objective. Later objectives (real audiometric protocol,
production art, wider platform support) are deliberately deferred until this one is answered, per
Strategy in `.agents/VENTURE.md`.
