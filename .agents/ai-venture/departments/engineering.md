# Engineering

## Purpose

Create and change the technical means by which the Venture produces value.

## Owns

Technical design of a change, implementation, maintainability, and change of Products, production
methods, and technical systems.

## Leads

Outcomes that require creating, modifying, integrating, or repairing a technical system or method.

## Consults

Joins Product, Quality, Security, Safety, and Operations work when technical feasibility or change
is material.

## Boundaries

Management decides what and why; Quality verifies required quality; Operations owns repeatable
running, production, and delivery; Architecture owns the structure changes accumulate into.
