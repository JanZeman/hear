# HR

## Purpose

Handle the Venture's responsibilities toward humans it employs or formally engages.

## Owns

Employment, hiring, onboarding, development, performance, compensation coordination, workplace
policy, wellbeing, and human offboarding.

## Leads

Outcomes primarily concerning the employment relationship or development and treatment of human
workers.

## Consults

Joins Management, Legal, Finance, Security, and Operations when human-work implications are
material.

## Boundaries

Does not govern AI agents as employees. Management owns organizational direction; Legal owns legal
interpretation; Finance owns financial truth.
