# UX

## Purpose

Make Products understandable, usable, accessible, and coherent for their beneficiaries.

## Owns

User experience, interaction, information architecture, accessibility, usability evidence, and
application of visual identity within a Product.

## Leads

Outcomes primarily concerning how someone understands, navigates, interacts with, or experiences a
Product.

## Consults

Joins Product, brand, consent, support, and Engineering work when the beneficiary experience is
materially affected.

## Boundaries

Marketing owns brand and public identity; Management owns Product direction; Engineering owns
technical implementation.
