# Management

## Purpose

Keep the Venture justified, directed, prioritized, and accountable to its Purpose and Goals.

## Owns

Purpose, Vision, Strategy, Goals, Roadmap, Product scope, priorities, pricing strategy, resource
allocation, and Venture-wide trade-offs.

## Leads

Decisions about what the Venture should do, why, for whom, in what order, and whether a material
initiative should proceed.

## Consults

Joins work whose consequences materially change direction, commitments, priorities, Products, or
cross-Department risk.

## Boundaries

Does not own the specialist truth or implementation of every decision. There is no default middle
management layer; specialist Departments Lead outcomes inside their boundaries.
