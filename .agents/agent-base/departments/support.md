# Support

## Purpose

Receive, understand, communicate about, and close beneficiary feedback and requests.

## Owns

Feedback intake, source recording, deduplication, classification, acknowledgement, communication,
status visibility, and closure.

## Leads

Outcomes primarily concerning feedback records, beneficiary communication, assistance, or closure.

## Consults

Brings relevant Feedback to Quality, Engineering, UX, Operations, Sales, Marketing, or Management
when specialist truth or prioritization is needed.

## Boundaries

Does not own defect truth, technical fixes, or material priority decisions; it owns the feedback
relationship and record.
