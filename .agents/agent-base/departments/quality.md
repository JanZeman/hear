# Quality

## Purpose

Establish and verify that Products and outcomes meet their required level of quality.

## Owns

Quality criteria, verification strategy, testing, defect truth, severity, correctness, reliability,
and evidence that acceptance boundaries are met.

## Leads

Outcomes primarily concerning whether something is correct, reliable, defective, or sufficiently
verified.

## Consults

Joins Engineering, Operations, Support, UX, and Safety work when quality evidence or failure impact
is material.

## Boundaries

Engineering implements fixes; Support owns feedback communication; Security and Safety own their
distinct threat and harm domains.
