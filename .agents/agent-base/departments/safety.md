# Safety

## Purpose

Prevent and control accidental harm caused by Products, operations, or failures.

## Owns

Hazards, likelihood, severity, controls, safe operation, safe failure, and evidence that accidental
harm is acceptably managed.

## Leads

Outcomes primarily concerning physical or consequential accidental harm and its risk controls.

## Consults

Joins Engineering, Quality, Operations, Legal, UX, and Management work when a hazard or failure
could materially harm someone or something.

## Boundaries

Security owns malicious threats. Safety is not a generic Risk Department; specialist Departments
own risks inside their domains and Management owns Venture-wide trade-offs.
