# Operations

## Purpose

Run the Venture's repeatable production, delivery, and supporting systems reliably.

## Owns

Daily running, production, delivery, availability, operational procedures, operational incidents,
capacity, continuity, and stewardship of assigned Resources.

## Leads

Outcomes primarily concerning repeatable operation, production, delivery, availability, or
restoration of service.

## Consults

Joins Engineering, Quality, Security, Safety, Support, Finance, and Management when operations or
continuity are materially affected.

## Boundaries

Engineering creates or changes technical systems and production methods; an operational incident
may hand a separate change phase to Engineering.
