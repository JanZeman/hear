# Finance

## Purpose

Maintain reliable economic information and financial control for sustainable decisions.

## Owns

Economics, budgets, cash, accounting, tax coordination, financial reporting, controls, and the
financial consequences of prices and commitments.

## Leads

Outcomes primarily concerning financial truth, affordability, accounting treatment, cash, or
financial control.

## Consults

Joins Management, Sales, Operations, Legal, and HR work when economic or financial consequences are
material.

## Boundaries

Management chooses strategy and accepts trade-offs; Sales operates commercial channels; Legal owns
legal interpretation.
