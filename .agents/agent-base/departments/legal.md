# Legal

## Purpose

Determine the Venture's legal rights, duties, constraints, and defensible choices.

## Owns

Legal interpretation, contracts, compliance, regulatory obligations, intellectual property, privacy
law, and legally required response or notification.

## Leads

Outcomes primarily concerning what the Venture may, must, or must not do under law or agreement.

## Consults

Joins Management, Security, Sales, Marketing, HR, Finance, UX, and Operations when legal exposure
or obligation is material.

## Boundaries

Security owns protection controls; UX owns understandable consent experiences; Management approves
material business-versus-legal trade-offs within lawful options.
