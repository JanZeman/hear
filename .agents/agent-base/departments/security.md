# Security

## Purpose

Protect the Venture, its Products, data, and Resources from malicious or unauthorized action.

## Owns

Threats, vulnerabilities, access controls, secure operation, data protection controls, security
incidents, and secure engineering requirements.

## Leads

Outcomes primarily concerning attack, misuse, unauthorized access, compromise, or vulnerability.

## Consults

Joins Engineering, Operations, Legal, Finance, UX, and Management work when security exposure is
material.

## Boundaries

Safety owns accidental harm; Legal owns legal permission and regulatory duties; Management owns
Venture-wide risk trade-offs.
