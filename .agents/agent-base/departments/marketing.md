# Marketing

## Purpose

Understand markets and communicate why the Venture and its Products matter.

## Owns

Market research, audiences, positioning, brand, public visual identity, awareness, demand, and
acquisition communication.

## Leads

Outcomes primarily concerning who should notice, understand, trust, or desire a Product and how it
is publicly represented.

## Consults

Joins Management, UX, Sales, Support, and Legal work when market meaning or public communication is
material.

## Boundaries

Sales operates commercial channels and conversion; UX owns Product experience; Management owns
Product and pricing strategy.
