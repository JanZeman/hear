# Sales

## Purpose

Enable relevant beneficiaries to acquire the Venture's Products through suitable channels.

## Owns

Commercial channels, marketplace and store listings, offers, qualification, transactions,
negotiation, contracts in the selling flow, closing, and channel execution.

## Leads

Outcomes primarily concerning how an approved Product, offer, or price is presented and transacted
through an acquisition channel.

## Consults

Joins Management, Marketing, Finance, Legal, Support, and Operations when commercial execution or
channel constraints are material.

## Boundaries

Management owns Product and pricing strategy; Marketing creates positioning and demand; Finance
owns economic truth; Legal owns legal interpretation.
