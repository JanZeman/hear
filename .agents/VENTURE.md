## Purpose

HEAR exists to give people an honest, low-friction way to notice changes in their hearing, by
disguising a real hearing screening as a short game genuinely worth playing on its own merits,
never requiring a clinical or stigmatizing framing to get there.

## Vision

A small, working, cross-platform prototype that people genuinely enjoy playing, whose result
feels meaningful and trustworthy enough that players take it to heart, despite it never claiming
to be a medical test.

<!-- The future state this Venture intends to create or reach. Prefer one sentence. -->

## Strategy

Prove the shared engine/shell architecture with a small number of prototype worlds before
investing in production art or the real audiometric protocol. Always disclose, with explicit
player acknowledgment, that this is not a medical device. Treat the whole thing as a prototype
until it is demonstrated to real people; if that demonstration doesn't generate interest, stop
rather than continue on momentum.
