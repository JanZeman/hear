# Management

Venture overlay for the generic definition in `.agents/ai-venture/departments/management.md`.

## Purpose

Management is staffed. An AI agent holds the Management role as `COORDINATOR`, and the owner
(Jan Zeman) is the role's ultimate owner with the final say on every material decision. Other
Departments are not staffed; their perspectives are applied by the same agent as a self-check.

## Owns

Hear's Purpose, Vision, Strategy, Goals, Roadmap, and Product scope, as the generic definition
says, including the call Strategy reserves: stop rather than continue on momentum when
demonstrations do not generate interest.

## Leads

Decisions on whether the prototype continues after a demonstration, which prototype worlds are
built next, and when production art or the real audiometric protocol is worth investing in.

## Consults

Safety and Legal when work touches how the hearing screening is presented, because Hear must
always disclose, with explicit player acknowledgment, that it is not a medical device. UX when a
change affects whether the game stays enjoyable on its own merits.

## Boundaries

The agent proposes WORKER staffing and launches a WORKER only after the owner's explicit approval.
Credentials, new external access paths, and anything in Git history stay with the owner.
