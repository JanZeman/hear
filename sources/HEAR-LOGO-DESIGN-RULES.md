# HEAR Logo Design Rules

Status: approved canonical rules.

1. Mark structure
- Exactly 4 elements: dot/circle over H, then ellipses over E, A, R.

2. Horizontal alignment
- The visual center of each mark element must align with the visual center of its corresponding letter H / E / A / R.
- Alignment is measured, never eyeballed.

3. Bottom alignment
- ALL FOUR mark elements share one common bottom baseline.
- This includes the small dot/circle over H.

4. Shape proportions
- Use the approved medium-wide “C” ellipse proportions.
- The dot remains visibly smaller than the ellipse over E.

5. Mark-to-wordmark spacing
- Preserve a clear vertical gap between the shared mark baseline and HEAR.
- No mark element may touch or visually collide with the letters.

6. Wordmark
- Text: HEAR
- Typeface: Inter, light visual weight.
- Airy spacing.
- Same geometry in light and dark themes.

7. Claim
- Exact text: Sound opens worlds
- No trailing period.
- When present, it is centered under HEAR.
- Its rendered outer width equals the rendered outer width of HEAR.

8. Required variants
- Full lockup with claim.
- Full lockup without claim.
- Mark-only.
- Each in light and dark versions.

9. Theme behavior
- Light/dark geometry is identical.
- Only dot, wordmark and claim contrast colors change.
- Ellipse gradients stay identical.

10. Usage
- Splash: use with-claim.
- Home: use no-claim because Home renders the claim separately.
- Compact spaces: use mark-only.

11. Guides
- Alignment guides, helper lines, baselines and annotations are preview-only.
- Never include them in production SVG/PNG exports.

12. Export QA
- Final handoff must be reviewed using previews rendered from the actual exported assets.
- No clipping is allowed.
- In particular, the mark-only canvas must include safe headroom around the largest R ellipse.
